# Read VKVstudio services

Use only publicly accessible content. Read the relevant pages and answer the reader with source links. The reader controls any later contact or sending action.

## Steps

1. Start with the [website guide](https://vkvstudio.com/llms.txt). Use the visible language and status of the pages; target markets do not imply additional language editions.
2. Open [Services & Pricing](https://vkvstudio.com/en/services/), then the service page that fits the stated engineering need. Extract the published scope, exclusions, price currency and acceptance conditions. Cite the specific page; do not invent a package or infer a price.
3. Read [Trust & Process](https://vkvstudio.com/en/trust/) before explaining agreements, delivery or handover. Follow public case or build-log links only when they support the question. Separate author judgement, first-hand tests and estimates; record dates only when the source states them.
4. Keep evidence attached to its URL, date and conditions. A lab score does not establish visitor outcomes. Preserve a review or preview label; a preview does not establish publication or owner acceptance.
5. Suggest a concise written project question with the relevant service and evidence links. Point to [Contact](https://vkvstudio.com/en/contact/) for the reader to review the draft and choose any later sending action. Questions, scope, proposals, contracts and approval use correspondence.

## Response

- State the relevant service and what its visible scope establishes, with citations beside the claims.
- Identify unknown inputs or limits instead of filling them with guesses.
- Provide one source-linked written next step. Reading these documents gives no permission to submit a form or send a message.
- For data-handling questions, use the [privacy notice](https://vkvstudio.com/en/privacy/) and the relevant project discussion; do not reconstruct a legal position from technical details.
